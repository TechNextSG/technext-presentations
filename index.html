Presenting tips
  • Builds before slides. On slides with steps, the first presses reveal each step; the next press moves on.
  • Click the demos. Maps, pipelines, toggles and the Odoo buttons in the walkthrough all respond to clicks; click empty space to advance.
  • Jump anywhere. Press O for every slide at once, or type a slide number and press Enter.
  • Leave it running. Add ?kiosk to the address (the “Kiosk loop” link) for an event screen that plays on its own.
Presenting on a TV or projector

Every deck runs in any of these set-ups. Pick the Display mode with the screen button in the deck’s control bar, or press T.

HDMI or USB-C cable

The sharpest picture. Press Win+P and choose Second screen only, open the deck and press F. Display: HDMI cable, full quality.

Notes on your laptop

Cable, with Win+P on Extend. Press P in the deck: your notes, the next slide and a timer open in a second window. Drag the slides to the TV and press F.

Wi-Fi casting

Chrome menu, Cast, Cast tab (Chromecast, Google TV), Win+K (Miracast) or AirPlay on a Mac. Display: Wi-Fi / casting keeps the stream sharp.

Zoom, Teams or Meet

Share the browser tab or window, not the whole screen. Wi-Fi / casting keeps it smooth for people watching remotely.

The TV’s own browser

Open the deck link on the TV (2021 models or newer): the remote’s arrows and OK move the slides. On older TVs, cast from the laptop or open the PDF.

Bluetooth clicker

Bluetooth carries the clicker, not the picture. Next and back work with any clicker, F5 starts full screen, B or . blacks the screen out.

No internet at the venue

Open each deck once while online, or press the button below: it then opens with no connection on this device. The PDFs and the Drive copy work offline too.
