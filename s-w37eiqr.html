project tiers

Four tiers for your Odoo rollout.

Tier 1

Starter

Getting started with one Odoo app.

On quotationafter discovery

  • 1 focus app (Accounting or Invoicing)
  • Odoo Online, standard setup
  • Company, taxes & users configured
  • 1 team training session
  • Basic data import (contacts, products)
  • 14 days go-live support
Tier 2

Essentials

Small teams moving off spreadsheets onto one system.

On quotationafter discovery

  • 1–3 focus apps (Accounting, Sales, Inventory)
  • Single company, standard configuration
  • Chart of accounts & core setup
  • Role-based team training
  • Data import (opening balances, master data)
  • 30 days go-live support
Most popular Tier 3

Growth

Growing SMEs connecting sales, stock and finance.

On quotationafter discovery

  • Accounting + Sales + Inventory + CRM + Purchase
  • Bank feeds & payment gateway integration
  • Light customisation & tailored reports
  • Full data migration
  • Multi-user, role permissions
  • 60–90 days support
Tier 4

Enterprise

Multi-branch groups needing the full suite.

On quotationafter discovery

  • Full Odoo suite, multi-company & multi-currency
  • Custom modules & advanced workflows
  • AI integrations (assistants, automation)
  • Dedicated project manager
  • Third-party & marketplace integrations
  • SLA support / annual maintenance

Odoo licences are billed by Odoo, per user per month. The tiers cover TechNext's implementation on Odoo 20; final pricing is confirmed per quotation.

Growth is the tier most growing SMEs start from: five apps connected, bank feeds and payment gateways, and a full data migration. "Choose" puts a tier into the plan on slide 6.
