part 03 · inquiries

From inquiry to quotation, in a minute.

    No payment is taken on the website. Your team confirms dates and price first, then sends the official quotation.

    Illustrative · sample traveller and reference numbers

    How an inquiry travels. Traveller: three short steps (what they need, trip details, their details); on Send, a quotation appears on screen straight away with a reference like ICQ-261015-4821, the details, an estimate when the package has a published rate, inclusions and terms. The same inquiry arrives in the agency's Gmail inbox with the reference in the subject, and the traveller gets a copy. They can save it as a PDF or send it on Messenger. Partner: travel agents, companies, schools and LGUs get a partner code from the agency; it opens a one-page partner form (quotation type, route, dates, adults, children and tour leaders, rooms, hotel class, net or commissionable rates, currency) with its own ICB reference. Nothing is priced on the page. Never show a real partner code on screen.
