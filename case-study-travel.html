This is the knot. The Hokkaido tour was priced in February at S$4,280 a head, 24 pax, at 90 cents per 100 yen: an 18% margin on the quote. Watch what happens by September: four guests cancel and the yen strengthens to 94 cents. The coach, the guide and the tour leader cost the same whatever the pax count, and every yen line costs more. The margin falls from 18% to about 11%, below the agency’s 12% floor, and the line turns red. Drag the rate and change the pax yourself. why it is hard

Suppliers are paid before the guests pay.

Deepest gapS$42,858163 days below zero

Four currencies, one price

The guest pays a fixed SGD price. Deposits go out in yen in March, balances in September, each at that day’s rate.

Costs land in the wrong tour

The coach company bills two departures on one invoice. Half of it lands in HKD-1027, half in “general”.

The last bill comes late

The guide invoices five weeks after the group lands. Until then the margin is a guess.

Illustrative · sample payment terms

Why is this structurally hard, and not just a spreadsheet problem? Three reasons. First, money moves in the wrong order: the agency pays the airline and hotel deposits in March, the second hotel deposit and the coach deposit in June, and the air tickets in August, but the guests pay a deposit at booking and the balance 45 days before departure. With a 10% deposit this one tour is under water from March to the end of August, S$42,858 at the deepest. Switch to 20% or 30%: the gap shrinks but does not disappear. Multiply that by a season of departures. Second, four currencies, each payment at its own day’s rate. Third, costs land in the wrong tour, and the last bill arrives weeks after the trip. Sample terms only: every supplier contract is different.

Illustrative · sample data

The answer is one idea: every departure is a project in Odoo, with its own analytic account. Everything that touches the tour carries that account: the sales order for each booking, the purchase orders and bills to hotels and coaches, the tour leader’s expenses. The project dashboard then shows revenue and costs side by side, expected and billed. Click each of the eight apps around it. Honest limits: Odoo is not an airline or hotel booking engine and not a GDS. Air tickets still come from the agency’s GDS or consolidator; in Odoo they are recorded as purchases against the departure. Passport copies in Documents sit in a restricted folder, collected only as needed under the PDPA.

Illustrative · sample data

Press Play or step through one booking. A couple enquires on the website and CRM opens an opportunity. The quotation comes from the tour’s quotation template, with an optional section of add-ons the guests set themselves in the customer portal. They sign and confirm online. The deposit is a down-payment invoice for 10%. On 30 August, 45 days before departure, the balance invoice is created from the same sales order and the deposit is deducted automatically. It isn’t paid on time, so a follow-up reminder goes out, and it is paid two days later. Every line carries the departure’s analytic account, so receipts land in HKD-1027. Honest note: the balance invoice is created by the team, or by an automation we set up; a down payment is not the same as an instalment payment term, and we pick the one that fits the agency’s terms.

Illustrative · sample rates and documents

Now the supplier side, on the coach for HKD-1027. Press the arrow. The purchase order is in yen, ¥1,650,000, and the project dashboard counts it as an expected cost in Singapore dollars. In September the bill is posted at 0.94: the dashboard moves it from expected to billed, the margin drops to 11.4%, and it is visible a month before departure, while there is still time to act. Then it is paid at 0.955. Drag the payment-day rate: Odoo posts the difference in the Exchange Difference journal as a foreign exchange gain or loss. Exchange rates can be fetched automatically every day. Honest note: realised exchange differences post to the company’s gain and loss accounts; if the agency also wants them per departure, we confirm that setup in discovery. Purchase orders only count on the dashboard once their bill is linked to the project’s analytic account.
