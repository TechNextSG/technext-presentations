what it is

Business apps that share one database.

A suite of business apps

CRM, Sales, Inventory, Accounting, Manufacturing, HR, Website and more. Each is an app you switch on when you need it.

On one database

Every app reads and writes the same customers, products and accounts, so nothing is typed twice.

Open source at the core

Odoo Community is open source. Odoo Enterprise adds apps, hosting options, upgrades and Odoo's support.

A new version every year

Odoo ships one major version a year and presents it at Odoo Experience in Brussels.

2005Starts as TinyERP
ThenRenamed OpenERP
2014Becomes Odoo
2026Odoo 20, at Odoo Experience

Built by Odoo S.A., a software company from Belgium. Implemented in Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam by partners such as TechNext.

Four ideas: modular apps, one database, an open-source core with a paid Enterprise edition, and a yearly release cycle. Odoo 20 was presented at Odoo Experience in September 2026.

editions

Community and Enterprise, side by side.

Odoo CommunityOpen source

  • Core business appsCRM, Sales, Inventory, Invoicing, Purchase, Manufacturing, Website, eCommerce, Point of Sale, Project
  • Free to useunder the LGPLv3 licence
  • You host iton your own servers or cloud account
  • Upgrades are yours to runwith your partner or your IT team
  • Community modulesfrom the Odoo Apps Store

Odoo EnterpriseSubscription

  • Everything in Communitythe same core apps, on the same database
  • Plus Enterprise appssuch as Accounting, Studio, Helpdesk and Sign
  • Hosting optionsOdoo Online, Odoo.sh or your own servers
  • Odoo's upgrade service and supportincluded in the subscription
  • Priced per user per monthOne App Free, Standard and Custom plans

Plan names and prices change from time to time and differ by country: check odoo.com/pricing for current terms.

Toggle "Only the differences" to fade what the two editions share. TechNext implements both; most growing companies choose Enterprise for Accounting, Studio and the upgrade service.
