                new in odoo 20

                What changed in Accounting, and where you saw it.

                Bill line prediction New

                A bill's product, account, tax and analytic distribution are suggested from past bills and the label.

                Invoice reminders New

                Follow-ups from the invoice list or the Send button, or automatic reminders set in the Accounting settings.

                Matching rules and auto reconciliation New

                Rules set the payment tolerance and matching order; Run Auto Reconciliation reruns them on demand.

                No double payments New

                The payment dialog finds payments already on their way and deducts them from the amount due.

                Payment statuses renamed Changed

                A payment that was "In Process" now shows Paid, and one that was "Paid" now shows Reconciled, once it meets its bank line.

                Parent accounts Changed

                Parent accounts replace account groups in the chart of accounts, and account codes become optional.

                Bank entries come from the bank Changed

                Every journal entry on a bank account must now come from a bank transaction, so manual mismatches cannot happen.

                Expenses arrive as draft bills Changed

                Approved expenses become draft bills in the expense journal; the separate Employee Expenses menu is gone.

                where we work

                Local rules, in the standard product.

                Singapore

                GST and the statements your auditor expects.

                • IRAS GST documentstax invoice, credit note and customer accounting PDFs, with GST taxes refined to prepare for InvoiceNow
                • SFRS statementschart of accounts, balance sheet and profit and loss improved to Singapore Financial Reporting Standards
                • PayNow QR codeson invoices, so customers pay from their banking app

                Philippines

                BIR formats, certificates and books.

                • BIR-format partner ledger and Books of Accountsfor CAS and CBA compliance
                • BIR 2306 and 2307 certificatesgenerated on vendor bills, and sent to the vendor when the bill is paid
                • BIR- and CAS-compliant invoicesplus the BIR 1600VT report and a disbursement voucher

                Vietnam

                Circular 99 and the 01/GTGT declaration.

                • Circular 99/2025/TT-BTCchart of accounts, balance sheet, General Journal S03a-DN and General Ledger S03b-DN
                • 01/GTGT with Appendix 142exported as XML for HTKK or the tax portal
                • SInvoice (Viettel) e-invoicessent from the invoice, and for Point of Sale orders and internal transfer notes
