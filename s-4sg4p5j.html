how the screening holds

The gate is the missing buy button.

Odoo will never read a questionnaire and then decide whether to allow a checkout. It will happily refuse to sell something that was never put on the shop.

Refused after paymentMoney taken, then refunded. Logic that has to be right every time.

Simply unavailableNothing to buy until you issue a quotation. The stronger legal position.

Good to know Booking links are public once published

Odoo has no hidden, link-only booking page. What protects a booking:

  • A secret URL
  • Your approval step
  • A product never published

Odoo has no link-only hidden booking page, so the controls are the approval step and not publishing the product, never the secrecy of a URL.
