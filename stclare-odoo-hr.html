        Illustrative · sample names, times and grace period

        Phase 2 replaces the three forms. On campus, a tablet at the gate runs Odoo's attendance kiosk: badge or PIN, and in Odoo 20 a device with a camera can take a photo at check-in. For online classes, teachers check in from inside Odoo. If St. Clare already has biometric devices, Odoo 20 connects eSSL and Mantra devices; any other brand we check first. Drag the arrival time: past the sample 10-minute grace period Ana turns late, and the division's board updates. Overtime goes to the approver, and Odoo 20 rulesets can carry public holiday rates. For one month the forms and Odoo run side by side; when they match, the forms retire.

        Illustrative · sample rates and deductions, not St. Clare's

        Phase 3 is payroll, and it only starts when attendance is trusted. Odoo 20 adds a basic Philippines payroll package: Form 1601-C items for monthly BIR reporting, the yearly 2316, 1604-C and Alphalist exports, and automatic loan deductions for SSS and Pag-IBIG loans. Switch between full-time and part-time and drag the hours: full-time pay grows with approved overtime, part-time pay with hours taught. All rates and deductions here are samples; St. Clare's own rates, contributions and tax are set up from your current payroll and checked line by line in two parallel cut-offs before the first real pay run. How part-time faculty are paid (per hour, per unit or per load) is one of our questions at the back.
