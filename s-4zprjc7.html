how an engagement runs

Five steps, one team from start to finish.

Step 1

Discovery

We map how orders, stock and money move today, match each step to an Odoo app and write the scope down.

You get: a written scope and fit-gap
Step 2

Configuration

Standard Odoo first, set up on a staging copy of your database. Custom modules only where the process genuinely differs, built to survive upgrades.

You get: a configured staging database
Step 3

Integration

Banks, payment gateways, online stores and the tools you keep are connected, so nobody re-types between systems.

You get: connected banks, payments and stores
Step 4

Training

Each team learns its own screens on a copy of your data, with a quick-reference guide for every task.

You get: trained teams and task guides
Step 5

Support

Fixes, month-end help, upgrades and small changes, handled by the team that configured your system.

You get: one support channel after go-live
