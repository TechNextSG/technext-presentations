before we start: the honest worries

“Will this break our books?” Here's the reassurance.

The worryThe reality

SQL Account is archived read-only, never deleted. Full audit lookup stays available.

Every migrated total is reconciled against your closing trial balance before anyone transacts.

One authoritative filing system per period. Migrated invoices are locked from re-sending.

Configuration and clean-up run alongside normal operations. Only the final cut is tied to the date.

Name the four worries out loud before they're asked. Press → (or click a worry) to answer each one. History is archived, not deleted; totals are reconciled before anyone transacts; one system files with LHDN per period; and the close carries on as normal.

the migration philosophy

One clean cut-off, one system of record.

We move balances and open items, not a blanket re-key of history. Account, customer and document codes carry forward.

risk register

Every risk has a control, and a go / no-go gate.

RiskA mid-period cut-over splits transactions

Cut over exactly at the close date, never before it.

RiskBank items or deposits left out of opening

Listed and signed off together with the aging.

RiskSST / e-Invoices filed from both systems

One authoritative filing system per period; no overlapping LHDN submissions.

RiskChart of Accounts mismatch

Mapping sheet reviewed and signed off before any import runs.

RiskMissing TINs cause MyInvois rejections

TIN and classification completeness checked in cleansing, before import.

RiskMigrated invoices re-sent to MyInvois

“Already e-invoiced” flag plus the stored UUID; the send action is blocked.

Go / no-go at the close
No-go0 of 5 checks passed

Owners named at every gate: the Finance Lead signs the mapping sheet and the reconciliation pack; the go / no-go at the close is joint, only when TB, aging, stock and bank reconcile and the MyInvois test has passed.

Six risks, each with its control. The gate on the right is the go / no-go: tick the five checks one by one (or "Run the gate"). It only turns to Go when all five pass, and the decision is made jointly with JR-Tech's Finance Lead.
