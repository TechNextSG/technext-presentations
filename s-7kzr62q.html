Straight talk: the BIR registers your system for your business; no provider can promise the outcome, and we never claim to be "BIR-accredited". What we promise is a complete, consistent pack and a team beside you until it is done.

Piece five: the CAS registration. Toggle who does what. The current framework is RMC 5-2021 and RMO 9-2021 (February 2021): the Acknowledgement Certificate replaced the old Permit to Use, there is no system demonstration or pre-evaluation, and the BIR issues the AC within three working days of complete documents; the application is a secondary registration in ORUS. The pack: system description summary, sample printouts of books, invoices and receipts, the printed audit trail, the forms and reports specifications, the signed Annex B standard requirements, and a sworn statement Moneybees signs. Compliance with Annex B is checked later, at post-evaluation or audit, so the pack must be accurate, not just complete. Tax advisers also read the move from official receipts to invoices under the EOPT Act as a major enhancement that needs a new AC: worth checking if Moneybees already holds one. We still confirm the checklist with their RDO first. Be straight: the BIR registers the system for the taxpayer; nobody can promise the result, and we never call ourselves BIR-accredited. Sources: Grant Thornton PH on RMC 5-2021 and RMO 9-2021 (Jan and Mar 2021) and on EOPT and CAS (Jul 2024), checked 21 Oct 2026.
