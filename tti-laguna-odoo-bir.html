The number is the whole slide, and it is live: it counts the days from today to 31 December 2026. State it calmly. RR 26-2025 sets the date: covered taxpayers issue compliant e-invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RMC 98-2026, issued 22 September 2026, sets the working rules: a Permit to Issue (PTI) before the first e-invoice, and EIS Certification within six months of the PTI. An issued e-invoice can’t be deleted or changed: a credit note lowers an amount, a new e-invoice raises it. If the system is down, a BIR-authorised manual invoice is issued, then an e-invoice that refers to it. The system sends the required sales data to the BIR’s EIS. Click any dot on the line for its rule; the pin shows today. Who is covered: sellers through e-commerce or internet transactions, Large Taxpayers Service, large taxpayers (RA 11976, RR 8-2024), and CAS or CBA users with invoicing software; micro taxpayers are exempt; head office and branches are covered. Click a group to mark it for the accountant to check: we do not decide which group TTI Laguna is in, their accountant or RDO confirms. General guidance, not tax advice. The BIR has not published a penalty for the 31 December date itself and the Commissioner can still extend it. We are not the BIR and do not decide penalties; their accountant or RDO confirms what applies. No scare talk: the point is to start early.
