your request for proposal

We can build your brief, line by line.

Every requirement in your RFP, and how we would build it. We have already tried each one on a WordPress staging build.

Requirement 2.1

Classic WordPress, as asked

header.php, index.php, single.php, page.php, footer.php, sidebar.php: the classic template files. No block theme, no theme.json, no full-site editing.

Your editors use the Classic Editor and Classic Widgets only. Gutenberg is switched off by the site's own plugin.

Requirement 2.2

A GeneratePress child theme

"HST Medical" runs as a child of GeneratePress, so the parent can update safely. Site facts live in Appearance › Customize, not in code.

Comes with an editor guide: where to change a product, the promotion band, the stores, the menus and the widgets.

Requirement 2.3

EasyCart, styled to fit

All 51 products in WP EasyCart, pack sizes as option items, Singapore checkout. Product, cart and checkout pages styled without breaking EasyCart's own layout.

EasyCart's 1.1 MB of scripts load only on store screens, not on every page.

Requirement 2.4

Fast on a phone on 4G

One stylesheet, one small script, one self-hosted font, no carousels. Every image has its size, so nothing jumps.

            The same three questions, asked of the rule-based assistant and of an AI assistant. The left column is the prototype's real answer today. The right column is illustrative: how a language-model assistant, restricted to your labels and with safety rules, would answer. First, a medicine question: rules find the catalogue caution but still show the product to buy; the AI recognises a question about mixing medicines and hands over to a pharmacist.
