                new in odoo 20

                What changed in CRM, and where you saw it.

                Lead distribution New

                Each member is always in rotation, in rotation with a limit, or out of rotation.

                Lead Sourcing with Dun & Bradstreet New

                Generate suggests more ways to find leads, and searches Dun & Bradstreet's database instead of Clearbit.

                Pipeline switcher New

                A dropdown beside the pipeline switches between the sales teams' pipelines.

                Activities for a person or a role New

                Assign an activity to someone or to a role, from a clearer Schedule Activity window.

                Mail plugins New

                From Gmail or Outlook, find the contact and log the email in its chatter.

                Upsells from a lead New

                See a customer's subscriptions from the lead and start upsell or renewal quotes linked to it (needs Subscriptions).

                Company identifiers New

                DUNS numbers and country company IDs on contacts, each checked against its type.

                Contacts before addresses Changed

                Address details are hidden on leads, so the team creates a proper contact instead.
