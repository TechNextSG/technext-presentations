The rules in plain words. RR 26-2025 sets the date: covered taxpayers issue compliant e-invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RMC 98-2026, issued 22 September 2026, sets the working rules: a Permit to Issue (PTI) before the first e-invoice, and EIS Certification within six months of the PTI. An issued e-invoice can’t be deleted or changed. Who is covered: e-commerce sellers, Large Taxpayers Service, large taxpayers, and CAS or CBA users with invoicing software; micro taxpayers are exempt. For a PEZA enterprise like Laguna Metts the group is the open question: published guidance puts PEZA registrants in a later group unless they are Large Taxpayers or CAS users, and zero-rated export invoices are still in scope. Click a group to mark it for the accountant to check: their accountant or RDO confirms which applies, not us. General guidance, not tax advice. Source: technext.asia/bir-e-invoicing.
