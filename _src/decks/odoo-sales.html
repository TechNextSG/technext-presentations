                new in odoo 20

                What changed in Sales, and where you saw it.

                Sales dashboard New

                Performance and quick filters at the top of the quotations and orders lists.

                Templates and sections New

                Pick a template as the quote opens; save sections as templates, with subsections and Hide Price options.

                Editable margins, periodic pricing New

                Edit a line's margin to recompute its price; pricelist rules for set dates or days of the week.

                Fixed prepayments New

                Ask for a fixed deposit amount instead of a percentage; the portal page is redesigned.

                Lazada and TikTok New

                Orders, delivery slips and stock sync with both marketplaces, next to Shopee (also available from Odoo 19).

                Commissions on paid amounts New

                Plans based on the amount paid, and managers' or split commissions rolled up from the team.

                Product images on orders New

                Product pictures on sales orders and their PDFs, and numbered order lines on the form, PDF and portal.

                Lines without a product New

                Order lines can be a description only; the Mandatory Product setting enforces a product where you need one.

                where we work

                Selling across the region, from one Sales app.

                Singapore

                GST on every line, paid by PayNow.

                • {{icon:receipt}}9% GST from the tax settingson every quotation line, refined in Odoo 20 to current requirements
                • {{icon:qr}}PayNow QR codeson the invoice, so the customer pays from their banking app
                • {{icon:cart}}Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shopall three connectors are documented for Singapore

                Philippines

                BIR-compliant invoices from the same order.

                • {{icon:file}}BIR- and CAS-compliant invoicesfrom the Philippine localisation in Odoo 20
                • {{icon:users}}Senior citizen and PWD discountsthe statutory discounts on invoices and credit notes
                • {{icon:bank}}Xendit and AsiaPaythe payment providers Odoo names for the Philippines

                Vietnam

                E-invoices and QR payment.

                • {{icon:send}}SInvoice (Viettel) e-invoicessent from the invoice with Send to SInvoice
                • {{icon:qr}}VietQR codeson invoices, for payment from a banking app
                • {{icon:cart}}Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shopall three connectors are documented for Vietnam
