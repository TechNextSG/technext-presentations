The rules in plain words. The day counter sits over 31 December; click any dot on the line. RR 26-2025 set the deadline: covered taxpayers issue compliant e-invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RMC 98-2026, issued 22 September 2026, sets the working rules: a Permit to Issue (PTI) before the first e-invoice, and EIS Certification within six months of the PTI. Four rules matter for day-to-day work: an issued e-invoice can't be deleted or changed; a credit note lowers an amount and a new e-invoice raises it; the system sends the required sales data to the BIR's EIS; and when the system is down, a BIR-authorised manual invoice is issued, then replaced by an e-invoice that refers to it. Below the line, who the rules cover: sellers through e-commerce or internet transactions (whatever their size), Large Taxpayers Service taxpayers, large taxpayers under RA 11976 and RR 8-2024, and CAS or CBA users with invoicing software; micro taxpayers are exempt and the Commissioner may add others. Cotton Central sells online, so the e-commerce group is the one to check, but their accountant or RDO confirms it, not us. The day counter uses today's date. Source: technext.asia/bir-e-invoicing, which cites RR 26-2025, RMC 98-2026 and the BIR advisory of 14 Sep 2026. We are not the BIR; their accountant or RDO has the final word.
