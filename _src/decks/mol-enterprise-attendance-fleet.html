attendances

Clock in at the depot, in seconds.

A tablet at the gate runs Odoo’s kiosk: scan a badge or tap a name. The hours land on each person’s record.

Press → to check the next crew member in, or click any name on the kiosk to check them in or out (the sample clock moves on with each click). The board on the right is the Attendances list: check in, check out and worked hours, per person.

What the chips mean, in Odoo 20’s own words: Kiosk Mode can be Barcode/RFID, Manual Selection, or both. Employee PIN Identification asks for a personal PIN. Extra Hours Validation is either Automatically Approved or Approved by Manager, with tolerances in favour of the company or the employee. Automatic Check-Out closes a shift someone forgot to close.

Office staff can also check in from the attendance icon in the top bar; that check-in logs the IP address and, with permission, GPS coordinates. Do not promise mobile GPS tracking of drivers on the road: Attendances records check-ins, not routes.

fleet

Every truck, its driver and its costs.

Each vehicle carries its plate, driver, odometer and every service, so the cost of one truck is one click.

The board uses Odoo’s four vehicle stages: New Request, To Order, Registered, Downgraded. Click any vehicle to open its record on the right: License Plate, Driver, Registration Date, Last Odometer, Location and Fleet Manager are the field names in Odoo 20. A Future Driver field plans the next hand-over (Apply New Driver).

Press → once: a service is logged on SMP 1021 (preventive maintenance, with the odometer reading), and the total moves. Service types are theirs to define (fuel, tyres, registration, insurance); a service can also come from a vendor bill in Accounting.

Odoo also has an End Date Contract Alert for leases and contracts, and Cost and Odometer reports by vehicle and by driver. All plates, names and amounts here are samples.

how they connect

One person, one record.

The driver who clocks in at the gate is the same employee on the truck. Nobody keeps a second list.

PCPaolo CruzDriver · Employees · sample

Three builds, or click a box. One: the attendance of the employee. Two: the same employee is the Driver on the vehicle record, so a change of driver is a change on one record. Three: service costs that arrive as vendor bills sit in Accounting, the app from our BIR conversation, on the same database.

The point to land: one employee list for HR, the depot and the fleet. Payroll is not part of this deck; if they ask, say we confirm the Philippine payroll scope in discovery.
