Each app writes to the same database, so a confirmed order is instantly a delivery for the warehouse and an invoice for finance. Click through the apps; it cycles on its own.

If asked what Odoo is: business apps built by Odoo S.A., a software company from Belgium. It started in 2005 as TinyERP, was renamed OpenERP and became Odoo in 2014. Odoo Community is open source; Odoo Enterprise adds apps, hosting options, upgrades and Odoo's support. One major version ships each year; Odoo 20 was presented at Odoo Experience in September 2026.

new in odoo 20

Odoo 20, in six changes.

{{odoo:ai_app:34}}New

AI agents

Agents create and update records, answer questions about a file and run from automated or scheduled actions. Every AI feature uses Odoo credits.

{{icon:plug}}New

An MCP connector

Outside AI tools connect to your Odoo database through MCP, the Model Context Protocol.

{{icon:phone}}New

Offline mode

Create, edit, archive and delete records without a connection, and rerun earlier searches.

{{odoo:industry_fsm:34}}{{icon:arrow}}{{odoo:planning:34}}Moved

Field Service is now in Planning

The Field Service app is discontinued. Its live map, routing, travel fees, website form and worksheets live in Planning.

{{odoo:accountant:34}}Changed

Accounting

Parent accounts replace account groups, account codes become optional, and bill lines are suggested from past bills.

{{odoo:hr_payroll:34}}Changed

Payroll

A dashboard built around warnings, test print of pay runs and net-to-gross simulation. Work entries are removed.

If asked about editions: Odoo Community is open source (LGPLv3), and you host and upgrade it yourself with your partner or IT team. Odoo Enterprise adds apps such as Accounting, Studio, Helpdesk and Sign, a choice of hosting, and Odoo's upgrade service and support, priced per user per month (plans and prices differ by country: check odoo.com/pricing). Most growing companies choose Enterprise.

If asked where it runs: start on Odoo Online, where standard apps and configuration cover most first phases. Choose Odoo.sh when a requirement needs custom Python modules; go on-premise only for a concrete IT policy or data rule. A database can move later; we plan and test that move on a copy first.
