Presenting tips

  • Builds before slides. On slides with steps, the first presses reveal each step; the next press moves on.
  • Click the demos. Maps, pipelines, toggles and the Odoo buttons in the walkthrough all respond to clicks; click empty space to advance.
  • Jump anywhere. Press O for every slide at once, or type a slide number and press Enter.
  • Leave it running. Add ?kiosk to the address (the “Kiosk loop” link) for an event screen that plays on its own.
