TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner. We are not affiliated with, accredited by or acting for the BIR or any government agency, and we do not file or register on your behalf. Your accountant or RDO confirms what applies to you.

Two jobs. BIR compliance: we go through the rules with the client and set Odoo up; the 2307s, SLSP, SAWT and QAP come from Odoo's Philippine localisation, from the entries the client already posts. Odoo has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and we have not built one: if a client is covered, we assess the options and quote any connection separately.

Business transformation: one database for the whole business, so nobody re-types between systems.
