TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner. We are not affiliated with, accredited by or acting for the BIR or any government agency, and we do not file or register on your behalf. Your accountant or RDO confirms what applies to you.

Regulatory compliance (BIR CAS), as on technext.asia: we set Odoo up for the client's own CAS or CBA application and prepare the sample printouts with them; the business registers at its RDO. 2307s, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and the 2550Q come from the invoices and bills already posted. E-invoicing is a separate project: Odoo has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and TechNext has not built one; the client applies for its Permit to Issue.

Business process improvement: one Odoo database runs the whole chain and TechNext configures every hand-off (the five steps are an illustration, not a client's figures).

ERP/CRM end-to-end implementation: discovery, configuration on a staging copy, data migration, training, integration and support; Accounting, Sales and Inventory first.
