Welcome everyone. This is the formal kickoff of the Odoo Accounting implementation with BIR CAS and EIS compliance for ATOMY Philippines. Kickoff today also closes Milestone 1 (Mobilization) once the project plan is agreed.

The flight path is the plan in one line: kickoff today, configuration complete 4 Dec, UAT sign-off 7 Dec, CAS and EIS documents 18 Dec, go-live and handover 29 Dec, two days before the 31 Dec BIR e-invoicing deadline.
