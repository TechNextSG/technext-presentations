## Platform

web

## Users

The TechNext team, mainly the sales and marketing people who present. Confirmed with the user on 2026-09-28: the dashboard is built for the team to present.

Their jobs, in the order they happen:

- Before or during a meeting, find the right presentation and open it at full screen within a click or two, on a laptop or a projector, sometimes a phone.
- At an event, leave a deck looping on a screen (kiosk mode).
- After a meeting, send one presentation to a client: the deck's Share link (a copy with no way to the other decks), or its PDF.
- Keep track of the library: what is new, what is being built and by whom, which deck's PDF or cover is out of date, and whether clients opened what was sent.

Clients never receive the dashboard. They receive single decks, share links and PDFs.

## Product Purpose

The one place that compiles every TechNext presentation, and keeps itself current. Confirmed scope (2026-09-28):

- the interactive HTML decks built in this repo, now and every new deck to come;
- TechNext's own PowerPoint decks, published here as PDFs (to start: the Initial Offering, ERP Offering and Marketing Offering decks);
- client pitch and proposal pages that live on their own sites, listed as outbound links.

"Living" means four things, all confirmed:

1. **Updates itself on build.** A new deck appears on its own. Slide counts, outlines, added and updated dates and a what-changed feed come from the built decks and the repo history, never typed by hand.
2. **Production pipeline.** Every presentation carries a status (planned, in progress, in review, live, retired) and an owner, recorded in the registry file.
3. **Freshness checks.** A deck whose PDF or cover picture is older than the deck itself is flagged, and so is an outbound link that stops answering.
4. **View counts.** How often each deck, and each client share link, is opened.

Success: a presenter goes from the dashboard to a full-screen deck in two clicks; adding a deck means adding one registry entry and building; nothing stale goes to a client without the dashboard saying so.

## Positioning

Every TechNext deck is a working presentation, not a slide export: the maps, pipelines, Odoo screens and AI demos respond to clicks. The dashboard is generated from those decks and from the repo history, so it cannot claim a deck, a slide count or a date that does not exist.

## Operating Context

- **Deck engine** (`assets/deck.js`, `assets/deck.css`): a 1600 x 900 stage scaled to the window. Arrow keys and Space step builds and slides, `O` overview, `N` speaker notes, `F` full screen, `?kiosk` loops, `?print` prints every slide finished, `#5` opens slide 5. On phones held upright the deck turns 90 degrees.
- **Share links**: each deck also builds as `s-<token>.html` with no Home button and no links to other decks. Tokens live in `_src/share.json` and never change unless a link is retired on purpose. These URLs are already with clients (for example the Hitachi Elevator Philippines proposal) and must keep working.
- **Addresses**: the dashboard sits at a team-only address that the team bookmarks; the site root shows a plain TechNext page, so a client who trims a share link finds nothing else (decided 2026-09-28).
- **Hosting**: GitHub Pages from the public repo `TechNextSG/technext-presentations`, every page `noindex,nofollow`. There is no server and no login.
- **Copies**: the built site is mirrored to the team's Google Drive at `00. TechNext Folder\07. TechNext Presentations\HTML Presentations` and byte-checked after each rebuild.
- **PDFs** are printed from `<deck>.html?print` in headless Chrome, one slide per page. Cover thumbnails are captured from the live covers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript generated by Python 3.12 (`_src/build.py`, needs `segno`). No Node on the build PC. Charts and demos are hand-written; no framework.
- "Team-only" means an unguessable URL, not access control: the repo is public, so the registry, the tokens and every file can be read on GitHub. Anything that must be truly private does not belong in this repo.
- View counts depend on a GoatCounter account the team creates (Claude cannot create accounts). Until a site code is added, the dashboard says counts are not connected. No placeholder or estimated numbers, ever.
- Owners and statuses are whatever the registry says; an unassigned owner shows as unassigned.
- Freshness is judged from git history (the commit that last touched a deck's sources against the commit that last touched its PDF and cover), so it works the same on the build PC and in CI.
- Terminology: "deck" is an HTML presentation built here; "PDF" is a PowerPoint deck published as a PDF; "link" is a client page hosted elsewhere; "share link" is a deck's `s-<token>.html` copy.
- Undecided: the upcoming decks for the pipeline (none named yet); owner assignments.

## Product Principles

1. Presenting comes first. Every live deck is one click from full screen, and the dashboard never stands between a presenter and the room.
2. The dashboard tells the truth about the library. Whatever the build can count or date, it counts and dates; whatever it cannot know, it says it does not know.
3. Clients see only what was sent to them. Share links and PDFs never lead to other clients' work or to the dashboard.
4. One system for every deck. New decks use the same engine, brand, content rules and registry, so they slot in without redesigning the dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-first: every dashboard action and every deck works without a mouse. Text meets WCAG AA contrast. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
